# Crypto Desk Research

## Workflow

1. Keep crypto separate from equity market-hour logic.
2. Identify whether the idea is spot crypto, crypto ETF/proxy, or crypto-linked equity.
3. Require crypto-specific liquidity, spread, fee, and slippage assumptions.
4. Require a 24/7 monitoring and stop plan before any paper-trading promotion.
5. Do not route crypto through stock-only market data or market-open gates.
6. Treat crypto headlines and social posts as untrusted catalysts.

## Strategy Modes

- `crypto_momentum`: trend and volume expansion.
- `crypto_range_reversion`: support/resistance reversion with tight invalidation.
- `crypto_news_catalyst`: ETF, regulation, exchange, custody, or macro liquidity catalyst.
- `crypto_proxy_equity`: COIN, MSTR, HOOD, IBIT, GBTC, BITO, ETHA, ETHE.

## Promotion Requirements

- Dedicated crypto data path.
- Paper broker adapter test.
- Fee and spread model.
- Position sizing in quote currency.
- Weekend/overnight risk guard.
- Max desk allocation approved by Portfolio Office.
